Language models learn to read, and sometimes write, text that is tokenized the wrong way, although nobody trained them to. This matters for alignment: adversarial re-tokenization can bypass guardrails, and the extra freedom in tokenization is a channel for hidden messages. The agenda, written with Sami Wolf under Dmitry Vaintrob, covers how invariant models are to re-tokenization, consistency training, and tokenizer design.
