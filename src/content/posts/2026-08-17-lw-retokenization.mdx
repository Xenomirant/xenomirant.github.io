LLMs develop the capacity to read, and sometimes write, non-canonically tokenised text, and nobody asked them to. We think this is understudied from an alignment perspective: adversarial re-tokenisation bypasses guardrails, and the extra degrees of freedom are a steganography channel. The agenda covers reader and writer invariance, consistency training, tokenisation curvature in in-context learning, entropy-regularised exploration in RL, and some tokenizer-design theory. Written as MARS V fellows under Dmitry Vaintrob; some of this may not hold at frontier scale.
