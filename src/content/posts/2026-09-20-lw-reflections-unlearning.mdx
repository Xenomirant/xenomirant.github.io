I see no evidence that inoculation removes a behaviour from the weights rather than suppressing it. The post separates learning in context from learning in weights, and distinguishes three targets of unlearning (a feature, a data point, a set of weights), following Xu and Strohmer. A simple counterexample shows that selective inoculation is impossible once the clean and harmful distributions overlap.
