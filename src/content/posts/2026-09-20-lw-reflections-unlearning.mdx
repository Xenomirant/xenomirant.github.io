I see no reason, theoretical or empirical, to believe that inoculation results in unlearning. The post separates learning in-context from learning in-weights, and feature, data-point and weight-anchor unlearning from each other, following Xu and Strohmer. There is a simple counterexample where selective inoculation is impossible once the distributions overlap. Adapters are task vectors with better marketing. I doubt genuine unlearning is possible in any foundation model, but I might be wrong.
