AI Safety Connections is a Connections-style word game for AI safety vocabulary, which Jamilya Erkenova and I built at Monoid. It began as a prompted prototype and now runs on puzzle-generation algorithms over a configurable vocabulary, with three difficulty levels, multiplayer and leaderboards. Students in Monoid's evals course played it weekly. Next we test language models as solvers.
