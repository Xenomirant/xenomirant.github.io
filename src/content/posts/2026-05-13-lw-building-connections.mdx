Building connections is hard. We started with a prototype prompted out of DeepSeek at a Monoid hackathon, then replaced the prompting with actual puzzle-generation algorithms over a configurable vocabulary, three difficulty levels, multiplayer and leaderboards. It ran weekly in Monoid's evals course and students rated it 4.5 out of 5. Next: models as solvers, a puzzle dataset, and looking at the reasoning traces.
