This is a personal site, which means it is a blog with a CV attached. Blogs were invented for people with graphomania who do not like Twitter. I have the first condition and the second opinion, so here we are.

The honest reason is practice. Writing is a skill that dies fast, and it dies faster when there is an agent willing to do it for you. Agents are convenient, and they are convenient to think against, but the line between thinking with one and outsourcing the thinking is thin and moves while you are not looking. The same goes for judging the result. Of everything a researcher does, judgment is the one skill that has not yet been made cheap, and I would like to keep it that way for as long as the market allows. Good texts are still hard to produce and still hard to find; people are still visibly tired of applications written by a model. So the plan is to write here, badly at first, and see whether it gets better.

The title comes from a Telegram channel I keep for the same purpose. A random walk on the line returns to its starting point with probability $1$; in three dimensions it does not, and in practice the dimension of the space you are searching is unknown. Pseudorandom is the best I can promise. The search terminates, usually.

<Aside label="note">
A human and an agent built this site. The agent wrote most of the code and none of the paragraphs. Where a model co-authored something I put my name on, the credit is on the page, as it is in the hackathon report under projects.
</Aside>

What goes here: notes on tensors and optimizers, on tokenisation symmetries, on unlearning that is mostly suppression, on evaluations and what they actually measure. Cross-posts from LessWrong appear as stubs that send you there. Some things will be in Russian when the thought is funnier that way. Photographs of night lights, badly, on purpose.

Nothing is promised about frequency. Replies are pseudorandom, but the search terminates.
