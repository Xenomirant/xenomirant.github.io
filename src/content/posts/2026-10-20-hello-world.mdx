Hi. This is where I'll put the things I write: notes on my research and whatever else I'm thinking about, plus links to my LessWrong posts. Some of it will be in Russian.

Mostly I just want to write more. I've noticed I think less carefully when a model drafts for me, so this is a place to practice doing it myself.

The name comes from my Telegram channel. A random walk on a line always comes back to where it started. In three dimensions it might never come back. I don't know how many dimensions my interests have.

No schedule.
