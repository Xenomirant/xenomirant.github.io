Membership inference asks whether a given sample was in a model's training data. A classifier that never sees the target model reaches 98.6% AUC on VL-MIA-Flickr and 99.9% on WikiMIA-24, so those benchmarks measure distribution shift, not memorisation. Hessian-based detectors find members at roughly chance rate. Of the benchmarks we checked, only LAION-MI held up.
