Companion to the FiMMIA paper. A classifier that never sees the target model gets 98.6% AUC on VL-MIA-Flickr and 99.9% on WikiMIA-24, so those benchmarks are measuring distribution shift, not memorisation. Hutchinson estimates of the input-space Hessian trace detect members at roughly chance; the input Hessian is not even positive semi-definite on training samples. Of the sets we looked at, only LAION-MI looked trustworthy. Use hash-based splits.
