Inoculation is a way to train a model on data carrying a bad trait without the model absorbing the trait. In experiments on Qwen2.5-1.5B, learned inoculation (adapters, soft prompts, prefixes) was not consistently better than plain prompting, and it degraded under distribution shift. It did resist hidden backdoors better. On the TOFU benchmark, inoculation did not remove factual knowledge, so it does not unlearn.
