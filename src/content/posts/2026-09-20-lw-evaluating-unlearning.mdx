Short empirical evaluation to go with the reflections. Learned inoculation is not consistently better than prompting, degrades under distribution shift, and soft prompts overfit worst; learned methods do resist hidden backdoors better. Low-rank adapters keep traits separable where full fine-tuning couples them. On TOFU, inoculation fails at unlearning, as expected: factual knowledge has a long description length. Reverse KL throughout, because it is the stable one.
