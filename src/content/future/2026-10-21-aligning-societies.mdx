“Aligning societies with Claude” will be the last Anthropic paper before the IPO.

<More>

All agentic systems follow the same underlying principles. Developing methods to control increasingly agentic systems does generalize. We already see language models used as proxies for online A/B testing, as digital twins and as agents within simulated economies.

We will not only see lots of AI safety methods from [AI control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06942) repurposed for [surveillance](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12346): I expect other methods, from e.g. mech. interp, to be increasingly adopted at scale for personal monitoring.

Substantial drops in the computational cost of training and fine-tuning neural networks, their white-box mathematical nature and the amount of human-collected data have already made them an extremely cheap playground for experiments that would never pass an ethics committee (the [pain axis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16247) paper and the [AI torture](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/man-builds-ai-torture-chamber-103106113.html) experiments that followed). This is only the beginning of what we should be preparing for.

</More>
