After a while you realize that most conjectures in mathematics fall into two sets: “unproven, but assumed to be true, and thus used as if they were true” and “interesting ones that nobody has stated yet”.

Working on the second ones is useful. Proving the first is a very marginal advancement, especially if you neither understand the proof nor discover useful techniques along the way.
