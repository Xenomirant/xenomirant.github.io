tldr: 'Muon does steepest descent under the spectral norm, which only makes sense for matrices. Tensorion extends it to tensor-shaped parameters (convolutions, MoE blocks) with a linear minimisation oracle over a tensor norm ball on adaptively chosen unfoldings; order two recovers Muon exactly. It converges faster than Adam and the tensor-aware baselines on the tasks we tried, which is what every optimizer paper says.'
